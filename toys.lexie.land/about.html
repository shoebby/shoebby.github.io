About VisunovOS

VisunovOS was a game-making operating system originally developed by Visunov, a Polish-American company that lived and died during the dot-com bubble. Its goal was to bring 'interactive storycraft' to PC users in an accessible and playful manner.

This is a web-based revival project by Alexandria van Eekelen, aiming to port this OS into the modern day through the power of the browser, while respecting the software principles Visunov espoused.

VisunovOS-WEB's (re)construction is kept extremely simple, consisting mostly of vanilla HTML, JS, and CSS. No NPM installs, as little reliance on libraries as possible, and no PHP. The entire website can be edited, runs locally and offline, and doesn't exceed 100 megabytes in size!

VOS is fundamentally a toolbox; something that contains a variety of intermingling, modifiable tools, and invites the addition of many more. As these features are more complex to reproduce in a web context, they might take a while to realize. Please be patient!

FAQ

What is VOS currently capable of?
This current version of VisunovOS-WEB (v0.00.kj.25.999) is still very incomplete, but at least there's a possibility for exporting Flippabook projects to zip. They can even be run on an Itch.io page!
A variety tools and toys have been added so far, recreating their features through diligent cross-referencing.
Current tools include:

DivBrush - A drawing toy that lets you draw with divs, including the many different CSS stylings that can be applied to them!
Flippabook - Import and jostle around various different files on multiple pages, whether it be a zine, a comic, or a visual novel!
Typist - Write, style, and export HTML text snippets through demonic incantations. Incantations are risky though, and can always go wrong...
Puppetshop - Construct posed puppets in dress-up game fashion and export them for further use!
NoiseBOXX - Write sounds and songs in the HTML grid!
Recurse^3 - A binary tree fractal creator. Each branch is a div, and can be styled as such!
Sierpinskinator - Sierpinski-esque fractal generator, add points to create various clouds of self-similar structure!
Pop-Up Collager - A toy to make collages out of pop-ups, with options to either display a blank page with injected HTML or a webpage of your choosing!
Poppy - The mascot of VisunovOS and your personal assistant. Albeit limited, her role in learning and playing with VisunovOS will grow with time!
Settings - Change some of VisunovOS's visuals to make it truly yours!
Spinny - The most-torqued OS of all time.
GuyMode - Need some guys?

How was VOS made?
VisunovOS is largely hand-written vanilla HTML, CSS, and JS. Some JavaScript libraries (like JQuery UI for draggable elements) are used as well, and of course all of this wouldn't be possible without knowledge hubs like the MDN docs, Stackoverflow, my webdev critters, and my wonderful partner. I hope to embed my love for hand-crafted work in VOS < 3

What JS libraries does VOS use?

  • HOWLER.JS - Specifically useful for sound sprites and managing overlapping, cut-off sounds.
  • JQUERY UI - VisunovOS is rife with draggable elements.
  • FILESAVER.JS - For saving Flippabook exports.
  • JSZIP - For zipping Flippabook exports.
  • STRINGIFY-CSS-STYLESHEET - Plays a major role in why DivBrush exports can even preserve their animations, thanks Tom!
  • COLORSTEPS.JS - A super useful snippet for creating color gradients, used in Recurse^3.
